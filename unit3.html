Lord Krishana

Krishna is a major Hindu deity worshipped as the eighth avatar of Vishnu and as a supreme god in his own right

Born in Mathura to Devaki and Vasudeva in a prison cell.

Raised in Gokul and Vrindavan by foster parents Nanda and Yashoda to hide from the tyrant king Kamsa.

Known for playful childhood acts like stealing butter and playing the flute to enchant the gopis
